# Credits

## Sounds

**Swing whooshes** (`swish_*`) are from **Swishes Sound Pack** by **artisticdude**
(https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack), released under **CC0** (public domain).

Changes made: converted to mono Ogg Vorbis; slowed to about 1.3x length (pitch kept); a very slight echo
accent and fade-out added; peaks normalised to -3 dB. `swish_heavy_*` come from the longer swishes, `swish_light_*`
from the mid-length ones, `swish_quick_*` from the shortest; `swish_blunt_*` are the shortest pitched down and
low-pass filtered.

**Hit sounds** (`sword_hit_*`, `heavy_hit_*`, `dagger_hit_*`) are from **Fantasy SFX Pack Vol 1** by **JC Sounds**
(https://opengameart.org/content/jc-sounds-fantasy-sfx-pack-vol-1), licensed under **CC BY 4.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made: converted from WAV to mono Ogg Vorbis and renamed (Sword / Heavy Sword / Dagger Hit Metal). `punch_hit_*` are derived from Heavy Sword Hit Metal: pitched down, low-pass filtered, shortened with a
fade-out, and normalised.
