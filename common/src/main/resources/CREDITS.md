# Credits

## Sounds

The weapon sounds are from **Fantasy SFX Pack Vol 1** by **JC Sounds**
(https://opengameart.org/content/jc-sounds-fantasy-sfx-pack-vol-1),
licensed under **CC BY 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made:

- Converted from WAV to mono Ogg Vorbis and renamed:
  - `sword_swing_*` / `sword_hit_*` (Sword Swing / Sword Hit Metal)
  - `heavy_swing_*` / `heavy_hit_*` (Heavy Sword Swing / Heavy Sword Hit Metal)
  - `dagger_swing_*` / `dagger_hit_*` (Dagger Swing / Dagger Hit Metal)
- `fist_swing_*` are derived from Dagger Swing: pitched down, low-pass filtered and amplified.
